## Evidence and interpretation limits

- The performance comparison comprises **15 accepted paired repetitions** (30 scored runs) across 50, 100, and 500 users, soak, and spike profiles. Faster aggregate p95 does not mean every gate passed: 500-user after p95 passed 0/3, after soak relative drift 1/3, and after spike recovery drift 0/3. The 50-user paired p99 and bulk-write p95 regressed.
- The reports compare distinct security, code-quality, and performance branches. Their results cannot be added together as a measured combined production release.
- The `final-report-gpt55-comparison` filename is historical; it does not establish a measured GPT-5.5-versus-5.4 experiment. No such result was invented.
- The operational review rubric table is a quantitative tool-gate matrix plus a qualitative human-review checklist. It is not evidence of a weighted or independently rated code-review study; the exact JMeter thresholds were this study's pre-scoring choices.
- The two billable Akamai test servers were deleted after the raw evidence archive was copied locally. The account balance at teardown was **$3.10 uninvoiced**, not a final invoice. A temporary API token remains pending separately authorized revocation.
